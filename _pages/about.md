I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Statistics at the University of Washington, where I am advised by Profs. [Alex Luedtke](https://www.alexluedtke.com/) and [Ting Ye](https://www.ting-ye.com/). I also work closely with Prof. [Masaaki Imaizumi](https://sites.google.com/view/mimaizumi/) at the University of Tokyo. Before coming to UW, I completed my M.S. in electrical and computer engineering at Duke University, advised by Profs. [Cynthia Rudin](https://users.cs.duke.edu/~cynthia/home.html), [Alex Volfovsky](https://volfovsky.github.io/), and [Sudeepa Roy](https://users.cs.duke.edu/~sudeepa/). Prior to that, I received my B.E. specializing in robotics and automation engineering from the University of Delhi, India, where I was advised by Prof. [Umang Soni](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=glxZ4TQAAAAJ&hl=en).

My research interests lie in causal inference, semiparametric efficiency theory, nonparametric and high-dimensional statistics, and deep learning. One of my recent works develops doubly robust estimation and testing for conditional distributional treatment effects. Another extends recent state evolution results under proportional asymptotics to bypass sample splitting in doubly robust off-policy evaluation, including when selecting the neural reward model.

I am currently working on (i) a rigorous active sensing framework for explaining AI predictions, (ii) causal inference for data-adaptive interventions on continuous exposures, with applications to proteomics, and (iii) optimal best-arm identification in encouragement designs.
